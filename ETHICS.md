## Labelling plan
1. **In the film:** a 4-second end card in Amharic and English: "ምስሎቹ፣ ድምፁና ሙዚቃው በAI የተሠሩ ናቸው / Made with AI: images, voice and music were generated by AI models", plus an "AI-GENERATED · በAI የተሠራ" badge.
2. **In the file:** the MP4 `comment` metadata states that it is AI-generated. Provider watermarks/provenance (e.g. SynthID in Google outputs) are not stripped.
3. **On the platform:** TikTok "AI-generated content" label and YouTube "altered or synthetic content" disclosure switched on when posting. The post text says it was made with AI.
4. **Style:** described by qualities (gouache, brush strokes, paper grain), never by naming a living artist.
